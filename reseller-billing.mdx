If you resell WhatsApp connections or phone lines to your own customers, Pilot Status bills **you**, and you bill your customers. This page explains when each charge happens on our side and what your system must record so both ends match: bill your customer before we bill you, and stop paying for customers who have left.

The full detail of each rule is in [Extra numbers & On Demand](/api/extra-numbers), [Phone line pricing and billing](/phone-lines-pricing) and [Plans](/plans). To automate it, see [Key types](/api/key-types), [Create workspaces](/api/workspaces), [Wallet and card via API](/api/wallet-and-card) and [Workspace webhooks](/api/workspace-webhooks).

## One workspace per end customer

**Keep each of your customers in a workspace of their own.** Billing, suspension and the wallet all work per workspace. If every customer shares your workspace, one failed renewal stops all of them at once; with one workspace per customer, a failed renewal suspends **only that customer's numbers**, and the others keep sending.

- **Create the workspaces through the API** with an [account key](/api/key-types#account-keys) (`ps_account_`, in **Profile → API**): [`POST /v1/workspaces`](/api/workspaces#create-a-workspace). You are the Owner of each one, and the response carries the workspace's primary key. Then act in any of them with the same account key and the `x-workspace-id` header.
- **Once the Pilot Status team marks you as a reseller, every workspace you create is born on Pay As You Go**, billed from the wallet, with **your reseller prices**: the per-number price in your contract and your phone line price. Nothing is charged at creation.
- **Each workspace has its own wallet, its own saved card and its own renewal date** (one month after it was created). Fund each one with [Pix or card through the API](/api/wallet-and-card), or link your shared card (below).
- **You can own up to 10 workspaces by default.** Over that, `POST /v1/workspaces` answers `409 WORKSPACE_LIMIT_REACHED` with the `limit`. Ask the Pilot Status team for a higher ceiling.
- **Workspaces you already had keep their plan.** Moving an existing customer to a workspace of their own means connecting their numbers there: a number cannot move between workspaces.

### Shared card

The Pilot Status team can link **your card** — the card saved on one of your workspaces — as the **shared card** of your customers' workspaces: once it is set on your account, every workspace you create inherits it, and the team can also link it to a workspace one by one. It is a backup, not the main payment method:

- **It pays only what the platform charges**: the monthly renewal, extra numbers, phone line purchases and renewals.
- **It is used only when the workspace's wallet does not cover the charge and the workspace has no card of its own.** A card saved on the customer's workspace always comes first.
- **It never pays a top-up.** Nobody in the customer's workspace can spend it on demand: top-ups are paid with Pix, the hosted page or a card typed in at that moment.
- The link is recorded in the workspace's **History** as **Backup card linked** / **Backup card unlinked**.

If you want a customer's failure to pay to suspend only that customer, **do not rely on the shared card for them**: with it, their renewals are charged to you.

## WhatsApp connections (numbers)

### When we charge

- **Creating a number or generating its QR / connection link does not charge.** The charge happens at the number's **first connection**. A number that never connected counts neither toward capacity nor toward billing. See [Charged at the first connection](/api/extra-numbers#charged-at-the-first-connection).
- **If a connected number goes over the capacity you already paid for, we charge the extra number right away**: the wallet first, then the saved card. The first charge is **prorated** to the days left in your cycle.
- **With no wallet balance and no saved card, creating a number over capacity is refused.**
- **A paid number is good for the whole cycle.** Deleting a number mid-cycle is not refunded, but the slot stays yours until the cycle ends: you can connect another number in it without paying again.
- **On official API (Meta) numbers, conversations and templates are billed by Meta**, straight to the WhatsApp Business account's payment method. That cost does not go through Pilot Status and is not included in any plan.

### The monthly renewal

Resellers are on the **Pay As You Go** plan: no plan fee, you pay per number. The price per number is the one in your contract, and every workspace you create inherits it.

- **On the renewal day, we charge every number that has ever connected and was not deleted**, including the ones that are disconnected on that day.
- **Disconnecting does not stop the charge. Only deleting the number does.** A number you delete before the renewal does not enter the next cycle.
- **There is no capacity to schedule or give back.** To pay less, delete the numbers you no longer want.

See [Pay As You Go works differently](/api/extra-numbers#pay-as-you-go-works-differently).

<Note>
  **On the On Demand, Basic, Premium and Enterprise plans the rule is different.** The renewal charges the plan plus the **paid capacity** in extra numbers, even if some of those numbers were deleted. To pay less, **schedule the reduction** with `DELETE /v1/subscription/extra-numbers` (or in the dashboard); it takes effect next cycle. See [Paid capacity is a 30-day entitlement](/api/extra-numbers#paid-capacity-is-a-30-day-entitlement).
</Note>

### When a payment fails

- **Extra-number charge refused at connection:** the number stays connected, but **sending from it is blocked** (`409 NUMBER_BLOCKED`, `reason: "extra_charge_unpaid"`) and the Owner is notified. Topping up the wallet or saving a card retries the charge on its own. You can also pay right away with **Pay now** on the **Numbers** page, or with [`POST /v1/numbers/{id}/pay`](/api/numbers/pay).
- **7 days unpaid:** the number is disconnected, and connecting it again counts as a new first connection, charged again. A Meta Cloud API number is never disconnected: it stays blocked for sending until it is paid.
- **Monthly renewal refused:** there is a **2-day grace period**, with a new attempt every 12 hours. After that, **every number in that workspace stops sending at once**: the API (`422 BILLING_SUSPENDED`), the dashboard chat, Chatwoot and the messages already queued. Numbers stay connected, **receiving keeps working** and inbound webhooks keep arriving. Phone lines are not affected: they have their own billing. Topping up the wallet or saving a card runs the charge again and releases sending.

  <Warning>
    **What gets suspended is the workspace, not one number.** If all your customers share one workspace and its renewal fails, all of them stop sending at once. Keep [one workspace per end customer](#one-workspace-per-end-customer): then a failed renewal suspends only that customer's numbers.
  </Warning>
- **15 days suspended**, on a plan billed from the wallet: the subscription is cancelled and the workspace goes back to the **Free** plan. The Owner is notified by email and WhatsApp. Numbers are not disconnected, but whatever goes over the Free limits is blocked.

### Restoring a deleted number (up to 30 days)

Deleting a number with `DELETE /v1/numbers/{id}` logs the device out right away, but the number stays **30 days in the trash**. Within that window it can be restored with [`POST /v1/numbers/{id}/restore`](/api/numbers/create) (or in the dashboard), with the same id, API keys, webhooks and history. On Pay As You Go:

| When you restore | Charge |
| --- | --- |
| **In the same cycle**, with its slot still free | **Nothing.** The paid slot is good until the renewal. |
| **In the same cycle**, but the slot was already used by another number | One extra number, **prorated** to the rest of the cycle. |
| **After the renewal** | One extra number, **prorated** to the rest of the cycle. The deleted number was not charged at the renewal and comes back over capacity. |
| A number that **never connected** | **Nothing.** It is charged at its first connection, like any other. |

- **The number comes back disconnected.** Connect it again (`GET /v1/numbers/{id}/connect`, or the Meta flow for an official number). That reconnection does not charge again.
- **If the charge does not go through**, the response is `422`, nothing is charged and the number stays deleted. Retrying the same restore never charges twice.
- **While the number is in the trash, the same phone cannot be created again** (`409 NUMBER_SOFT_DELETED`): restore it or purge it.
- **After 30 days the deletion is final** (`404 RESTORE_WINDOW_EXPIRED`). Purging before that is final too and, on a Meta number, deregisters it at Meta.
- The `number.restored` webhook tells you when a number comes back.

In practice: if your customer paid late, you deleted their number before the renewal and they paid afterwards, restoring costs the prorated rest of the cycle, and you keep their history and settings.

### What your system must record

- **Which workspace belongs to each customer** (its `id`, from `POST /v1/workspaces` or `GET /v1/workspaces`), and **which customer owns each number**, with the **date of its first connection**. The [`number.connected`](/concepts/webhooks) webhook is the trigger: that is when we start billing you, so that is when you start billing your customer. `number.disconnected` tells you when the number dropped.
- **Each workspace's renewal date** with Pilot Status. When one of your customers stops paying, **delete their number before that date**. Disconnecting is not enough. If you do nothing, you pay that customer's next month. (Outside Pay As You Go, delete the number **and** schedule the capacity reduction.)
- **A cancellation rule that follows ours.** We do not refund the cycle in progress. If you refund your customer, the difference comes out of your pocket. The paid slot stays yours until the cycle ends: use it for your next customer.
- **Who pays Meta**, if your customers use the official API: who owns the WhatsApp Business account and which payment method is on it. Put that in your contract with your customer.
- **Numbers blocked for non-payment:** read `extraChargeUnpaidSince` and `extraChargeDisconnectAt` on `GET /v1/numbers` to know what is blocked and when it will be disconnected. See [Pay extra number](/api/numbers/pay#how-to-know-a-number-is-blocked).

## Phone lines

### When we charge

- **R\$ 33,90 per month, per line** (US\$ 33.90 on dollar workspaces), on any plan, including Free — unless the Pilot Status team set **your own phone line price**. Then every workspace you create inherits it, and it is the price of every purchase and of every renewal from the next one on. See [Your own phone line price](/phone-lines-pricing#your-own-phone-line-price).
- **The purchase charges the whole first month**, with no proration.
- **Each line renews on the same day of the month it was bought**, independent of the plan's and the numbers' cycle. Five lines bought on different days give five renewal dates.
- **Lines use the same wallet as the plan and the numbers.** There is no balance reserved per product: whichever charge comes due first uses the balance first.
- **A line does not count toward the number limit.** If you activate WhatsApp on a line and connect that number to Pilot Status, it is billed like any other number, under the rules in the section above.

### Notices and overdue lines

- **About 48 hours before each renewal**, the holder and the Owner get a notice listing the lines that renew that day and the total.
- **Without a saved card**, there is also a notice when the wallet balance does not cover the renewals of the next 7 days.
- **Renewal refused:** the line stays **2 days** with payment pending (a new attempt every 6 hours), then it is **suspended** and, **1 day** later, **returned to the carrier**. See [Overdue lines](/phone-lines-pricing#overdue-lines).
- **Cancelling a line in good standing:** it keeps working until the end of the month already paid, with no refund, and is returned on that day.
- **Holder verification rejected:** lines bought while the verification was under review keep working for 3 days and are then returned. What was paid for them comes back as wallet balance.

<Warning>
  **A return cannot be undone.** There is no guarantee you can buy the same number again, and the line's code history is deleted. If a WhatsApp account was activated on the number, whoever rents it next can request a verification code and take over that account. Never let one of your customers' lines lapse without a decision.
</Warning>

### What your system must record

- **Which customer owns each line** and its **purchase date**, because it renews monthly on that date.
- **Bill your customer before the renewal date**, not after. Our grace period is 2 days, and the return is final.
- **Customer in default:** decide **before the line's anniversary** whether you cancel the line or pay one more month for them.
- **Useful webhooks** (see [Phone line webhooks](/api/phone-lines/webhooks)): `phone_line.purchased`, `phone_line.renewed`, `phone_line.payment_failed`, `phone_line.suspended`, `phone_line.returned` (with `reason`: `unpaid`, `canceled_by_customer` or `verification_rejected`) and `phone_line.canceled`. Each line also exposes `price` (what its next renewal costs), `amountDue` (what paying an overdue line now charges), `currency` and `currentPeriodEnd` through the [phone lines API](/api/phone-lines/list#line-object-fields). The same events reach a [workspace webhook](/api/workspace-webhooks).

## Balance

- **Keep in each workspace's wallet, at least**, the line renewals of the next 7 days **plus** the monthly renewal of its numbers.
- **Keep a saved card as a backup** — on the workspace, or your [shared card](#shared-card). It is what prevents blocked sending and returned lines when the wallet does not cover a charge.
- The Owner tops up the wallet in **Plans → Wallet**. Through the API: Pix or a hosted page with [`POST /v1/billing/checkout`](/api/wallet-and-card#top-up-with-pix), or a checkout inside your own app with [Stripe Elements](/guides/transparent-checkout). [`wallet.topup_paid`](/api/workspace-webhooks#paid-top-up-event) tells you when the money is in, so you can release your customer's service on your side.
