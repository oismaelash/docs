The **Ads** screen answers two questions: which ad each WhatsApp conversation came from, and who filled in the Instant Forms (Lead Ads) of your Facebook Pages. It never asks for a Meta login: access comes from **partner sharing** in Meta Business Manager, where the advertiser adds the Pilot Status business portfolio as a partner.

## How to get there

- Sidebar: **Ads** (marked **Beta**), keyboard shortcut `s`. Also in the command palette (`Ctrl+K`) as **Ads**.
- Path: `/ads`. The tab is in the address: `/ads` is **Conversations**, `/ads?tab=forms` is **Forms**.
- `/ads?ad=<ad id>` opens the screen filtered to one ad — that is where the ad badge of a conversation in the chat takes you. The old address `/anuncios` still works and redirects to `/ads`, keeping the filter.

## Who can do what

| Action | Owner | Admin | Agent | Analyst |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open the screen, read the per-ad counts | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Link / unlink an ad account, see the partner ID | ✓ | ✓ | — | — |
| Link / unlink Facebook Pages, configure forms, resync | ✓ | ✓ | — | — |
| See the received leads (personal data) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Delete a lead | ✓ | ✓ | — | — |

Without the right to link accounts, the connection card says **Only a workspace admin can connect the ad account. The counts above are still yours to read.** On the **Forms** tab, an Agent sees **Linking Pages and configuring forms is done by a workspace admin.**; an Analyst sees **No access to forms** — *Viewing leads requires chat access, and linking Pages requires integrations access. Ask a workspace admin.*

## Conversations tab

### Period and counters

At the top, **7 days**, **30 days** and **90 days** (30 days by default). Three counters add up the ads listed below: **Conversations**, **Ads** and **Messages**.

<Note>
  Ad attribution has no history. When the chosen window reaches back before the day attribution started, the screen says so: **Ad attribution started on {date}. Conversations from before that date do not show up here, even the ones that came from an ad: the origin was not being stored yet.**
</Note>

When the list is capped, **List cut short** says that only the ads with the most conversations are shown and that the counters add up that list alone — shorten the range to see the rest.

When filtered by an ad (`?ad=`), a chip **Filtered to one ad:** shows the ad id, with **See all ads**. If that ad has no data yet: **No data for this ad yet**.

### The ads list

One row per ad, ordered by conversations. Each row shows:

- the ad name, with campaign and ad set below — or **Ad with no name** with **Name not looked up yet** (not asked Meta yet) or **Name unavailable: this ad's account has not been shared yet**;
- **Ad ID** — the ad's `sourceId`;
- conversations, messages, **leads** (when lead capture is on) and **last on** (date of the latest conversation).

With nothing in the period: **No conversation came from an ad in this period**. For whoever can link accounts, it adds whether an account is linked; when none is, **See how to link** opens the instructions below.

### Connecting the ad account

Without a linked account, the card **Connect your ad account** shows how many conversations and ads came in during the period. With one, the card **Linked accounts** lists each account (name, or **Unnamed account**, and its `act_…` id) with **Unlink**. If the advertiser revoked the grant on Meta, the row warns **Partner access to this account is no longer active on Meta. Share it again to keep the names coming.**

Below it, **Link your ad account**:

1. **How to grant access** (expandable) lists the steps in Meta Business Manager:
   1. In Meta Business Manager, open **Users** and then **Partners**.
   2. Click **Add** and choose **Give a partner access to your assets**.
   3. Enter the partner ID shown below.
   4. Pick **Ad accounts** and check the **View performance** permission.
   5. Come back here, paste the ad account ID in the field below and click **Link account**.
2. **Partner ID (Pilot Status)** — the numeric ID of the Pilot Status business portfolio, with **Copy**. This is the value you paste in Business Manager. It is not a secret and is always visible here (Owner and Admin); it is also returned as `partnerBusinessId` by `GET /v1/ads/accounts`. If the installation has none configured, the screen says **The partner ID is not configured in this installation. Talk to support before going into Business Manager.**
3. **Ad account ID** — type the id as `act_` followed by digits (placeholder `act_1234567890`), exactly as in the advertiser's Meta Ads Manager, then **Link account**.
4. **Accounts shared with us** — appears only when there are accounts already tied to this workspace by ads it received; each has a **Link** button. **Refresh** re-reads the accounts and their sharing state.

Link errors shown under the field:

| Message | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **The ID must look like act_ followed by digits…** | Typo in the id. |
| **This account has not been shared with the Pilot Status portfolio yet…** | The partner grant has not reached Meta. Redo the partner step with the partner ID and **View performance**, then link again. |
| **We could not confirm the sharing with Meta right now…** | Meta did not answer; try again in a few minutes. |
| **The ads integration is not configured in this installation…** | Server-side configuration; talk to support. |

If the whole installation lacks Meta ads credentials, the banner **Ads integration unavailable in this installation** explains that no ad gets a campaign name and no account can be linked — sharing again in Business Manager will not help.

## Forms tab (Lead Ads)

### Facebook Pages

The **Facebook Pages** card (*Link a Page to receive the leads from its ad forms here.*) has:

- **Linked Pages** — each Page with its lead count, the subscription state (**Receiving leads since {date}**, **Subscription pending** or **Subscription failed**), **Number that receives the leads** (a selector), **Show forms** / **Hide forms**, **Resync** and **Unlink**.
- **Pages shared with us** — Pages shared with the Pilot Status portfolio but not yet linked: choose the number that will receive the leads and click **Link**.
- **No Page shared yet** — with **Our portfolio ID:** (the same partner business ID) to give the Page owner, who shares the Page in Business Manager with the ads and community permissions. **Refresh list** re-reads.

Rules and messages:

- A Page can only be linked when an **official Meta number** (Cloud API) of the business portfolio that owns the Page is connected. Otherwise: **No official Meta number connected**.
- If the Page was shared without the needed permissions, linking lists what to ask for (for example, *Ask the client to add the ads permission when sharing the Page.*).
- **Resync** asks Meta again for the leads; right after one: **A sync ran a moment ago. Wait a few minutes before trying again.**
- Changing the receiving number can turn auto-send off: **Auto-send turned off: the new number uses a different message type; review the forms.**

**Unlink** opens the confirmation **Unlink {page}?** — *This deletes the leads captured through this Page and stops receiving new ones. It cannot be undone.* — with **Cancel** and **Unlink and delete leads**.

### Forms and auto-send

**Show forms** lists the Page's Instant Forms with their status, **Auto-send on** / **Auto-send off**, **No WhatsApp consent checkbox** when applicable, and **Configure**.

**Configure** opens the dialog **Configure {form}** — *Choose whether the lead gets a WhatsApp message as soon as they fill in the form.*:

- **WhatsApp consent checkbox** — the form's checkbox that authorizes the message. Only leads who tick it get a message. A form without one shows **This form has no WhatsApp consent**: create a version of the form on Meta with that checkbox.
- **Send automatic message** — opens the WhatsApp conversation with every new lead who gave consent.
- **Template** (official numbers: only an approved template can start a conversation) or **Message** (free text for other numbers, with variables like `{{nome}}`).
- **Variables** — for each variable, **Source of {name}**: a form question or **Fixed text**.
- **Cancel** / **Save**. Save stays disabled while something is missing (consent, template or message, variable sources).

### Received leads

**Received leads** — who filled in the forms, newest first, 25 per page (**Previous** / **Next**). Filters: **Filter by Page** and **Filter by form**. Columns: **Date**, **Name**, **Phone**, **Form**, **Ad / campaign** (or **Organic (no ad)**), **Auto-send** (**Off**, **Queued**, **Sent**, **Not sent**, **Failed**), and the actions **Open conversation** and **Delete**.

With privacy (PII) mode on, name and phone show as redacted. **Delete** opens **Delete this lead?** — the lead's name, e-mail, phone and form answers are erased permanently; the chat conversation stays — with **Delete permanently**.

In the chat, a conversation that came from a form shows a badge **Came from the form {form}**, with the lead's e-mail and **Answers**.

## Do it via API / MCP

| Dashboard action | API | MCP tool |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Per-ad summary | `GET /v1/ads/summary` | `ads_summary` |
| List linked/candidate accounts, read the partner ID | `GET /v1/ads/accounts` (`partnerBusinessId`) | `ads_accounts_list` |
| Link an ad account | `POST /v1/ads/accounts` | `ads_accounts_link` |
| Unlink an ad account | `DELETE /v1/ads/accounts/{adAccountId}` | `ads_accounts_unlink` |
| List Pages (linked, candidates, partner ID) | `GET /v1/lead-pages` | `lead_pages_list` |
| Link a Page | `POST /v1/lead-pages` | `lead_pages_link` |
| Change the receiving number | `PATCH /v1/lead-pages/{pageLinkId}` | `lead_pages_update` |
| Unlink a Page (deletes its leads) | `DELETE /v1/lead-pages/{pageLinkId}` | `lead_pages_unlink` |
| Resync | `POST /v1/lead-pages/{pageLinkId}/resync` | `lead_pages_resync` |
| List a Page's forms | `GET /v1/lead-pages/{pageLinkId}/forms` | `lead_forms_list` |
| Configure a form (consent, auto-send) | `PUT /v1/lead-pages/{pageLinkId}/forms/{formId}` | `lead_forms_update` |
| List leads / one lead | `GET /v1/leads`, `GET /v1/leads/{id}` | `leads_list`, `leads_get` |
| Delete a lead | Dashboard only | Dashboard only |

The partner grant itself happens in Meta Business Manager; no endpoint does it. References: [Ads API](/api/ads), [Lead Ads Pages API](/api/lead-pages), [Leads API](/api/leads).
