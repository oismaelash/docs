Phone lines live under **Phone lines** in the sidebar, right below **Numbers** (keyboard shortcut: `i`). The item shows for **Owner and Admin**; Agent and Analyst do not see lines, because a line's page shows its WhatsApp activation code, and that code is a credential.

Buying, paying overdue lines, cancelling and returning are **Owner**-only actions. The full walkthrough, from the holder verification to typing the code into WhatsApp, is in the guide [Phone lines: buy one and use it on WhatsApp](/guides/phone-lines).

## Lines list

The **Phone lines** page (`/linhas`) lists the lines that are still yours — active, payment due, or suspended. A returned or cancelled line leaves the list.

At the top are **Line webhooks** and **Buy a line**, and a summary such as **3 lines · 1 needs attention**. The list starts with what needs action: suspended lines first, then payment due, then cancellation scheduled, and the rest by renewal date.

Each line shows its name (or its number, if it has no name), its area code, and one of these statuses:

| Status | What the line shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | The next renewal date and how many codes the line has requested. |
| **Payment due** | The date the renewal failed. The line still receives codes. |
| **Suspended** | The suspension date. The line does not receive new codes. |
| **Cancellation scheduled** | Until when the line stays active. |

A line's page can also show **Returned** and **Canceled**, the two final statuses.

### Notices at the top of the list

- **Unpaid lines** — a red notice (**One line needs payment**, or **… lines are suspended or unpaid**) says by when to settle, with **Add balance**, **Pay for all** and, when more than one line is overdue, **Choose which to keep**. See [Phone line pricing and billing](/phone-lines-pricing#overdue-lines).
- **Your verification is under review** — you can already use your lines normally while Pilot Status checks the document.
- **We need another document to finish the account holder verification.** — **Send document** takes you back to the verification.
- **The account holder verification was rejected.** — talk to support.

The verification notices only show for the Owner.

## Buying lines

**Buy a line** opens `/linhas/comprar`, in three steps:

1. **Verification** — only shows while the account holder is not verified: CNPJ or CPF, the holder's WhatsApp number, the document and the confirmation codes.
2. **Numbers** — pick the area code, click **Search numbers** and select up to 10.
3. **Confirm** — the price per line, the **Total now** and the **Buy** button. After the purchase, the result is shown per number, with **Open line** for each line bought.

Each step is explained in the [guide](/guides/phone-lines#step-by-step).

## Line page

Click a line to open its page (`/linhas/{id}`).

### Header

- **The line's name**, with the **pencil** (**Edit name**) next to it. The name is optional, up to 60 characters and, left blank, the line shows its number. With a name, the number shows right below it.
- **Area code · active since … · renews …** — the purchase date and the next renewal date.
- **The status** and, for the Owner, **Cancel line** (line in good standing) or **Return number** (overdue line). See [Cancel or return](#cancel-or-return).

### Payment due or suspended

A line with payment due shows **We could not charge … on …**, with the exact dates on which it will be suspended and the number returned, and the **Add balance** and **Pay … now** buttons. A suspended line shows **Line suspended for non-payment**, with the date the number will be returned, and the **Add balance** and **Pay … and reactivate** buttons.

<Note>
  **Pay … now** immediately retries the charge of **every** overdue line in the workspace, not just the open one — like **Pay for all** on the list.
</Note>

### Activation code

The code panel has the **Wait for a new code** button, which prepares the line to receive the WhatsApp call. It is disabled while a request is waiting or being read, and on a suspended line. After you click it, the panel goes through these states:

| What the dashboard shows | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting** — **Waiting for the call from Meta** | The line waits for the call for 5 minutes, with the **Time left**. In WhatsApp, tap **Call me**. **Cancel wait** ends the request. |
| **Reading** — **The call arrived: reading the code…** | The audio is being transcribed. You can listen to the recording meanwhile. |
| The code, with **Copy** | **Last code** and the time it arrived. Type it into WhatsApp. |
| **We got the call, but could not read the code** | Listen to the recording right below. |
| **No call arrived** | The window closed with no call. The panel lists the usual reasons and offers **Wait again**. |
| **Wait canceled** | The request was canceled. Click **Wait for a new code** before asking WhatsApp for the code again. |
| **We could not capture the call** | The call arrived, but the recording could not be stored. Ask for the code again. |

### Code history

Below the panel is the **Code history**: the 20 most recent requests, with **When**, **Code**, **Recording** (**Listen**) and **Status** — **Waiting**, **Reading the code**, **Received** (with the code, or with "—" when the call carried no 6-digit code), **Recording received** (the call arrived, but reading it failed), **Did not arrive**, **Canceled** or **Failed**. On a suspended line, the history is **read only**.

### Cancel or return

**Cancel line** and **Return number** open a confirmation that explains what happens to the number and asks you to type the line's number.

- **Cancel line** (line in good standing) schedules the cancellation for the end of the month already paid, with no refund. The page then shows **Cancellation scheduled: the line stays active until …**, with **Undo cancellation**.
- **Return number** (overdue line) returns the number to the carrier right away. You go back to the list, with a confirmation of the return.

Once the number goes back to the carrier, the line's page only says it no longer belongs to you.

## Choose which lines to keep

With more than one overdue line, **Choose which to keep** opens a list of the unpaid lines, the wallet balance and how many of them the balance covers. Check the ones you want to keep and click **Keep … and return …**:

- the checked lines are charged right away;
- the **unchecked ones are returned right away**, for good.

If a checked line cannot be charged, the dashboard says so; add balance and try again.

## Line webhooks

**Line webhooks** (`/linhas/webhooks`), at the top of the list, is the webhook registry of the Phone Lines product — separate from the number webhooks: a number webhook never receives line events. Owner and Admin manage it.

- **New webhook** — an optional name (up to 100 characters; left blank, it becomes the URL's host), the `https` URL and the events: **Every phone line event** or the ones you pick (**Line purchased**, **Call audio received**, **Code received**, **Code not obtained**, **Line renewed**, **Charge failed**, **Line suspended**, **Number returned**, **Line canceled**, **Verification updated**). A URL pointing to an internal or private-network address is refused on save.
- **Signing secret** — shown **only once**, right after creation. Keep it: deliveries are signed with the `x-pilot-status-signature` header. Editing the webhook does not change the secret, and there is no rotation; to change it, create a new webhook and delete the old one.
- **Edit webhook**, **Deactivate** / **Activate** and **Delete** — an inactive webhook shows as **Paused** and gets no deliveries; deleting stops deliveries to that URL right away.
- **Deliveries** — pick a webhook to see its most recent deliveries (up to 50): **When**, **Event**, **Status** (**Pending**, **Delivered**, **Failed**), **Response** and **Attempts**. Clicking a delivery opens its details, with the body that was sent.

The counters at the top show **Total Webhooks**, **Active** and **Inactive** — or, with a webhook picked, **Total deliveries**, **Delivered** and **Failed** for the deliveries listed.

Payloads, signature verification and retries: [Phone line webhooks](/api/phone-lines/webhooks).

## Lines under Plans

Under **Plans** (sidebar), two tabs show the money side of the lines:

- **Finance → Phone line charges** — the wallet balance, whether it covers the renewals of the next 7 days, each line's next renewal with who will pay it (**Wallet pays**, **Saved card pays** or **Balance too low**), and the **Charge history**, with period, status (**Paid**, **Failed**, **Pending**, **Refunded**), attempts and the next attempt. On the same tab, **Consolidated spend** breaks out what was spent on **Phone lines**, and **Receipts and invoices** has a **Payment proof** for every paid charge.
- **Wallet** — the wallet **Statement** has **All**, **WhatsApp** and **Phone lines** buttons to filter by product. Lines show up as **Purchase** (first month) and **Monthly fee** (renewals).

Details in [Phone line pricing and billing](/phone-lines-pricing).

## Who gets the notices

Line notices — a failed renewal, a suspension, a return, a balance that does not cover the upcoming renewals, and the outcome of a manual verification review — go to the **account holder**'s contacts given in the verification and to the workspace **Owner**. All of them go by e-mail; all but the failed-renewal one also go by WhatsApp — and only to a confirmed WhatsApp number. Other workspace members do not get them.
