title: "Delete line webhook"
sidebarTitle: "Delete line webhook"
openapi: "openapi.json DELETE /v1/phone-lines/webhooks/{id}"
---

<Warning>
  **This deletes the webhook for real**, with its delivery log. Its pending deliveries are discarded and nothing more is sent to that URL. See [Configure](/api/phone-lines/webhooks#configure).
</Warning>
