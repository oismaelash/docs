---
title: "Cancel phone line"
sidebarTitle: "Cancel phone line"
openapi: "openapi.json DELETE /v1/phone-lines/{id}"
---

<Warning>
  **This cancels the line for real.** An `ACTIVE` line is cancelled at the end of its period, with no refund. An unpaid line (`PAYMENT_PENDING`, `SUSPENDED`) is **returned to the carrier at once**, with no guarantee it can be bought back, and its code history is deleted. See [Cancel a line](/api/phone-lines/cancel).
</Warning>
