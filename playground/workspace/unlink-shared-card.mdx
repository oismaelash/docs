---
title: "Unlink the shared card"
sidebarTitle: "Unlink the shared card"
openapi: "openapi.json DELETE /v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/shared-card"
---

<Warning>
  **Your reserve card stops paying this workspace's renewals right away.** If its own wallet and card do not cover the next renewal, the workspace is suspended. Requires an account key (`ps_account_`) and the Owner role. See [Shared card per workspace](/api/workspaces#shared-card-per-workspace).
</Warning>
