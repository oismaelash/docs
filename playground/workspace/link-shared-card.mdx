<Warning>
  **This changes who pays this workspace's renewals.** Once linked, your reserve card pays the platform charges the workspace's own wallet and card do not cover. Requires an account key (`ps_account_`) and the Owner role. See [Shared card per workspace](/api/workspaces#shared-card-per-workspace).
</Warning>
