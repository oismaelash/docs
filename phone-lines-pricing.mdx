Phone lines are billed separately from the plan: each line has its own monthly price and its own renewal day. This page covers how much a line costs, when and how it is charged, and what happens if a renewal goes unpaid. To buy and use a line, see the guide [Phone lines: buy one and use it on WhatsApp](/guides/phone-lines).

## Price

| Workspace currency | Price per line |
| --- | --- |
| Brazilian real (BRL) | **R\$ 33,90 per month** |
| US dollar (USD) | **US\$ 33.90 per month** |

- **The same price on every plan, Free included, and for any quantity.** There is no volume discount and no annual billing.
- **The price and the currency are recorded on the line at purchase**: every renewal of that line charges that amount, in that currency.
- **A line does not count toward the plan's number limit.** It is not a connected WhatsApp number; it is the landline you activate one on.
- **No limit on lines per workspace.** Each purchase takes 1 to 10 numbers.

## When each line is charged

- **At purchase, the first month is charged in full**, with no proration.
- **Then every month, on the purchase day.** Each line has its own cycle, so lines bought on different days renew on different days. The next renewal date shows on every line (**renews …**) and under **Plans → Finance**.
- **A day that does not exist next month becomes the last day of the month, and stays that way.** A line bought on Jan 31 renews on Feb 28 (or 29), then Mar 28, Apr 28 and so on. Dates are computed in UTC.
- **The charge happens within about an hour after the renewal date**, because phone line billing runs every hour.

## How it is paid

1. **First, the prepaid wallet balance.**
2. **Whatever the balance does not cover goes to the saved card.**
3. **If the card part fails**, what came out of the wallet goes back to it, and the charge counts as unpaid.

Lines use the **same wallet** as the plan and the extra numbers. To add balance, the Owner uses **Plans → Wallet** (or **Add balance**, on the line notices).

<Tip>
  With a saved card, every renewal has somewhere to go when the balance runs out. Without one, Pilot Status warns you when the wallet balance **does not cover the renewals of the next 7 days** — one notice per renewal date, to the holder and the Owner. With a saved card, this notice is not sent.
</Tip>

When something goes wrong **after** the money moved — a purchase that could not be completed, or a renewal charged on a line that was being returned — the amount goes back **as wallet balance**, including any part paid by card.

## Overdue lines

If a renewal cannot be charged, the line goes down this path:

| When | Status | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Renewal day | **Payment due** | The charge failed. The line keeps working, new codes included. The charge is retried **every 6 hours**. |
| **2 days** after the failure | **Suspended** | The line stops receiving new codes; the history stays visible. The retries go on. |
| **1 day** after the suspension | **Returned** | The number goes back to the carrier. **Final.** |

From the first failed charge to losing the number takes about **3 days**. Each step happens within about an hour after its deadline, and each one sends a notice to the holder and to the Owner.

**To settle**, add balance (or save a card) and:

- click **Pay for all**, on the notice at the top of the lines list, or **Pay … now** / **Pay … and reactivate**, on the line's page. Both buttons immediately retry the charge of **every** overdue line in the workspace;
- or, with more than one overdue line, use **Choose which to keep**: the checked lines are charged right away and the unchecked ones are **returned right away**.

If you do nothing, the next automatic retry (every 6 hours) already uses the balance or card you added.

**Paying late keeps the renewal date.** When the charge finally goes through — even on a suspended line — it goes back to **Active**, and the new period counts from the original renewal date, not from the payment day.

<Warning>
  **A return cannot be undone.** The number is no longer yours, and there is no guarantee you can get the same number again. The WhatsApp account activated on it stays tied to the number: whoever rents it next can request a verification code and take over the account.
</Warning>

## Cancellation and refunds

- **Line in good standing (Cancel line):** **no refund**. The line keeps working until the end of the month already paid, is not renewed and, on that day, the number is returned. Until then, **Undo cancellation** reverts it.
- **Overdue line (Return number):** there is no paid period left, so the number is returned right away, and nothing else is charged for it.

Only the Owner can cancel, undo a cancellation, or return a line.

## Where to see the charges

Under **Plans**, in the sidebar:

- **Finance → Phone line charges** — the wallet balance, whether it covers the renewals of the next 7 days, each line's next renewal with who will pay it (**Wallet pays**, **Saved card pays** or **Balance too low**), and the **Charge history**: period, amount, how much came from the wallet and from the card, status (**Paid**, **Failed**, **Pending**, **Refunded**) and, for a failed charge, the reason, the date of the next attempt and, when there was more than one, the number of attempts.
- **Finance → Consolidated spend** — the total spent per month, with **Phone lines** broken out from the other products.
- **Finance → Receipts and invoices** — one row per paid charge, with the **Payment proof** (which records the payment but is not a tax invoice) and, when a card was involved, the Stripe invoice or receipt.
- **Wallet → Statement** — the **All**, **WhatsApp** and **Phone lines** buttons filter by product. The purchase shows up as **Purchase**, and the renewals as **Monthly fee**.

## Through the API

The public API exposes each line's `price`, `currency` and `currentPeriodEnd`, and the `phone_line.renewed`, `phone_line.payment_failed`, `phone_line.suspended` and `phone_line.returned` events. **Pay for all** and **Choose which to keep** exist only in the dashboard. See the [Phone Lines API overview](/api/phone-lines/overview#pricing-and-payment) and [Phone line webhooks](/api/phone-lines/webhooks).
